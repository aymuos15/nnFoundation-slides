# nnFoundation slides

Reading-group slides on *nnFoundation: 3D Foundation Models for Radiology* (Harsy, Wald, Gotkowski et al., 2026): [arXiv:2609.26924](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26924).

Presented by Lorena and Soumya.

- `slides.tex`: Beamer source
- `slides.pdf`: compiled slides
- `delta.dat`: per-dataset Dice differences used in one chart

Build with `latexmk -lualatex slides.tex`. The build needs the Inter and Literata fonts.

## Disclaimer

This is an educational summary made for a reading group. We are not the authors of the paper and claim no ownership of its work, results, or figures. All credit belongs to the original authors (mainly the DKFZ group). Numbers come from the paper and its supplement, and some were derived from its tables (geometric means, win counts).
